
Attached scripts for the manuscript: 
Genetic architecture of high-dimensional liver radiomic phenotypes and their role in common metabolic diseases 

From the method perspective, it contains the following major modules: 

1. Deep-learning MRI phenotyping 
	Stitching
	Segmentation
	Radiomics extraction
2. Epidemiological analysis
	MRI-internal analysis
	MRI-external-traits analysis
	MRI-incident-outcome analysis
3. Genome-wide association analysis and its related analysis
	GWAS (REGENIE) 
	BMI-adjusted GWAS
	Heritability and enrichment analysis
	Replication
4. Genetic factor analysis
	Cross-trait LDSC
	First-level SEM
	Second-level SEM
5. Locus pleiotropy 
	internal pleiotropy
	external pleiotropy (PLATLAS)
6. Multi-omics integration
	PRS and in-sample LD
	PWAS
	Colocalization
7. Genetic correlation
8. Radiomic MR
	MRI-similarity-clustering
	UVMR
	MVMR
	MVMR-SuSiE/MVMR-FA
9. Drug-target MR
	cis-MR (IVW, LD-IVW, PC-GMM)
	polygenic-MR (MR-Clust)
